# PRODUCT ASSUMPTIONS

**Team:** J&J  
**Members:** Jihan Abdul Rahman B, Joel Thomas Kampadukad  
**Inspiration:** Curate (Product Hunt)

## 1. What product are you planning to build?

We are planning to build a personal film archive for people who love watching films, especially arthouse, independent and world cinema. The platform will allow users to keep track of the films they have watched, add ratings and reviews, create their own lists, and record details about the edition or format they watched. They can also mention where and how they watched a film. The main idea is to have everything in one place, making it a personal film collection rather than a social media platform.

## 2. What problem does it solve?

Film records are usually scattered across different apps, notes, spreadsheets and sometimes just memory. Existing platforms like Letterboxd, Trakt and IMDb are useful for tracking films, but they don't provide a dedicated option to properly record the exact edition or format watched. As people who watch arthouse and international films, we wanted a platform where we could also keep track of the editions we own and watch, instead of just marking a film as watched. This platform aims to bring all these details together in one place.

## 3. Who are the target users?

The main target users are cinephiles between the ages of 18 and 35 who watch several films a week and enjoy arthouse, independent and world cinema in different languages. They may also be interested in directors, international cinema and film history. Some users might collect Blu-rays or other physical media and would like to maintain a detailed record of their collection. The platform is also intended for people who prefer keeping a personal archive rather than sharing everything publicly.

## 4. What is the main use case?

The main use case is adding a film to the archive immediately after watching it. Users should be able to do this in under a minute by entering details such as the film name, rating, a short review or personal note, the edition they watched, and where they watched it. They can also mention whether it was their first watch or a rewatch. Later, they can browse their library and find films using search and filters such as director, country, decade, edition and viewing location.

## 5. What are the core features?

The main features will include a library grid to display all the films in the archive, search and filters to find films easily, and individual film pages containing details such as ratings, reviews, editions and viewing information. Users will be able to add and remove films, create and manage their own film lists, and maintain a watchlist. There will also be a statistics dashboard showing information such as the total number of films watched, ratings, and films watched by country, decade and viewing location, such as home or theatre.

## 6. What assumptions are you making about the users?

We assume that users are comfortable entering film details manually as long as the process is quick and simple. We also believe that they care more about directors, countries and decades than just genres, and would prefer a private archive instead of a public profile. We assume that recording the edition and where they watched a film is important to them. Most users are also expected to access the platform through a laptop or mobile browser. However, we are not completely sure whether other people will find this idea useful. Since it is something we personally wanted, we would like to build it and get feedback from a few friends who are also interested in films to understand whether they would find it useful.

## 7. What will be included in the MVP?

The first version will include the main features needed to demonstrate how the platform works. Users will be able to browse the film library, search and filter films, view individual film details, add and remove films, create and manage their own lists, manage their watchlist and view the statistics dashboard. All these features will work using dummy data, without requiring real user accounts, a backend or live film data.

## 8. What will NOT be included in the MVP?

To keep the first version simple, we will not include real login or user accounts, backend or database integration, Letterboxd import, or social features such as following users, likes, comments and public profiles. Film recommendations, AI features, streaming availability and connections to external film databases or APIs will also be excluded. These features can be considered for future versions if needed.
